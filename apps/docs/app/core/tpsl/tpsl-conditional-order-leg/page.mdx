# TpSlConditionalOrderLeg

One leg of a conditional-order message body. Built by [`buildConditionalOrderLeg`](/core/tpsl/build-conditional-order-leg) and carried as the `takeProfit` / `stopLoss` field of a [`TpSlConditionalOrderMessage`](/core/tpsl/tpsl-conditional-order-message).

## Fields

<Properties>
  <Property name="quantity" type="string">
    Quote quantity (decimal string).
  </Property>
  <Property name="price" type="string">
    Slippage-shifted fill price (decimal string, rounded to market precision).
  </Property>
  <Property name="orderType" type="number">
    Wire `order_type` (`1` = market).
  </Property>
  <Property name="conditionalPrice" type="string">
    Trigger price (decimal string, rounded to market precision).
  </Property>
  <Property name="conditionalPriceType" type='"market" | "last_close"'>
    Wire form of `TpSlPriceType` — `market` for mark price, `last_close` for last-close.
  </Property>
</Properties>

## Related

- [`buildConditionalOrderLeg`](/core/tpsl/build-conditional-order-leg) — builds this.
- [`TpSlPriceType`](/core/tpsl/tpsl-price-type) — the SDK-facing price type mapped onto `conditionalPriceType`.
- The `ZERO_LEG` constant is a zero-value leg of this shape used to pad `null` legs before signing.
