# QuoteLifecycle

The stage of a quote's lifecycle as tracked across the on-chain and off-chain merge. Drives row styling and polling acceleration in the consumer. Enumerated so consumers can `switch` on it.

The off-chain stages (`OPTIMISTIC`, `PRICE_FILLED`), the `WRITE_ONCHAIN*` "awaiting the RPC" stages, and the transient close stages are **SDK-side overlays** — they do not exist on the on-chain [`QuoteStatus`](/core/symmio-contract/get-quote); reconciliation assigns them from notifications and hedger records.

```ts
enum QuoteLifecycle {
  OPTIMISTIC = "optimistic",
  PRICE_FILLED = "price-filled",
  WRITE_ONCHAIN = "write-onchain",
  ONCHAIN = "onchain",
  OPTIMISTIC_CLOSE = "optimistic-close",
  CLOSE_PRICE_FILLED = "close-price-filled",
  WRITE_ONCHAIN_CLOSE = "write-onchain-close",
  CLOSING = "closing",
  CLOSED = "closed",
  FAILED = "failed",
}
```

## Members

<Properties>
  <Property name="OPTIMISTIC" type='"optimistic"'>
    Submitted to a hedger; no on-chain quote and no fill report yet.
  </Property>
  <Property name="PRICE_FILLED" type='"price-filled"'>
    A hedger reported a fill price (`InstantRFQ` success) but the on-chain quote has not landed yet.
  </Property>
  <Property name="WRITE_ONCHAIN" type='"write-onchain"'>
    The open anchored on-chain per the notification (the `quoteId` is known), but the on-chain read has not returned the
    quote struct yet — shown with its anchored id while the SDK waits for the RPC. Resolves to `ONCHAIN` once the read
    returns.
  </Property>
  <Property name="ONCHAIN" type='"onchain"'>
    Anchored on-chain; mirrors a real `QuoteStatus`.
  </Property>
  <Property name="OPTIMISTIC_CLOSE" type='"optimistic-close"'>
    A close was just submitted and the hedger's pending-instant-close feed has it, but no close notification has
    confirmed a price yet — the close-side analog of `OPTIMISTIC`.
  </Property>
  <Property name="CLOSE_PRICE_FILLED" type='"close-price-filled"'>
    A close notification reported the close price/fill request (`InstantRequestToClosePosition`) — the close-side analog
    of `PRICE_FILLED`.
  </Property>
  <Property name="WRITE_ONCHAIN_CLOSE" type='"write-onchain-close"'>
    The close fill (`FillMarketOrderInstantClose`) was reported, or a pending instant-close overlays the row while the
    on-chain status still shows the position open — the close is being written on-chain, awaiting the RPC. Resolves to
    `CLOSING` once the on-chain status reflects the pending close.
  </Property>
  <Property name="CLOSING" type='"closing"'>
    The close is confirmed pending on-chain — on-chain `CLOSE_PENDING` / `CANCEL_CLOSE_PENDING`.
  </Property>
  <Property name="CLOSED" type='"closed"'>
    The position is fully closed.
  </Property>
  <Property name="FAILED" type='"failed"'>
    A hedger or notification reported the open/close failed.
  </Property>
</Properties>

The `WRITE_ONCHAIN*` and `CLOSING` stages are exactly the ones [`shouldAccelerateOnchainReads`](/core/quotes/should-accelerate-onchain-reads) treats as awaiting on-chain confirmation; every mid-transition stage above drives [`shouldAccelerateQuotePolling`](/core/quotes/should-accelerate-quote-polling).
