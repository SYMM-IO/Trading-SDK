# walkOrderbook

Fill a hypothetical market order against resting depth. A buy consumes asks from the best price outward; a sell consumes bids.

```ts
import { walkOrderbook } from "@symmio/trading-core";

const walk = walkOrderbook(book, "buy", 5);

if (walk.partial) console.warn("not enough resting depth");
console.log(walk.averagePrice, `${walk.slippageBps.toFixed(2)} bps`);
```

## Parameters

<Properties>
  <Property name="orderbook" type="Orderbook" required>
    The book to walk.
  </Property>
  <Property name="side" type='"buy" | "sell"' required>
    `buy` consumes asks, `sell` consumes bids.
  </Property>
  <Property name="size" type="number" required>
    Base-asset quantity to fill.
  </Property>
</Properties>

## Returns

<Returns type="OrderbookWalk">
  The fill, or a zero-filled result when `size` is not positive or the side is empty.
</Returns>

<Properties>
  <Property name="filledSize" type="number">
    Base-asset size actually fillable — less than requested when depth ran out.
  </Property>
  <Property name="filledQuote" type="number">
    Quote-asset cost (buy) or proceeds (sell).
  </Property>
  <Property name="averagePrice" type="number" optional>
    Size-weighted average fill price. Absent when nothing filled.
  </Property>
  <Property name="bestPrice" type="number" optional>
    Best price on the side walked. Absent when that side is empty.
  </Property>
  <Property name="worstPrice" type="number" optional>
    Price of the deepest level touched.
  </Property>
  <Property name="slippageBps" type="number">
    Cost of crossing the book, in basis points of `bestPrice`. Always `>= 0` — a buy averages above the touch and a sell below it, and both are reported as a positive cost.
  </Property>
  <Property name="partial" type="boolean">
    `true` when the book held less depth than requested.
  </Property>
  <Property name="levelsConsumed" type="number">
    Number of price levels consumed.
  </Property>
</Properties>

<Callout type="warning">
  Nothing but the book is considered — no fees, no funding, no solver spread. Treat the result as the cost of liquidity,
  not a quote. When the source's `priceBasis` is `reference-exchange` this is a third-party venue's depth, not what a
  SYMMIO trade executes against.
</Callout>

## Interpreting `partial`

A `partial: true` result may mean the venue is genuinely thin, or merely that the subscription is tracking fewer levels than the order needs. If you price large orders, raise `levels` on the subscription (or `limit` on the snapshot) before treating it as a liquidity signal.
